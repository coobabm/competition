# ReLU 可采用中英文文案

## 中文

1. 正式名称：整流线性单元（ReLU）
2. 通俗解释：ReLU 的原理是正数照传、负数归零；游戏用较高的层间保留比例表现它让深层网络更容易学习。
3. 真实效果：仅解锁购买条件不生效；购买一次后无需装备，两条训练线在随后轮次结算时获得处理量 ×1.3，与其他处理量倍率相乘，不重复叠加。每轮卡数取整并限制在 4–320 张，离线固定 12 张不享受此加成；轮次时长不变。自动配置优先采用 ReLU，一次只用一种激活函数；采用后，概念板每跨一层基础保留 98% 的反向学习信号、95% 的前向信号，逐层相乘，其他已生效技术可改变比例。若层数超过结构可承受层数，上限与过拟合估算中每个超出层的有效参数保留由 75% 改为 85%；未超出时此项无变化。成绩仍需训练后评估，不是立即加分。

## English

1. Name: Rectified linear unit (ReLU)
2. Explanation: ReLU passes positive values through and sets negative values to zero. The game represents easier learning in deep networks through higher per-layer retention.
3. Effect: Meeting the unlock requirements alone gives no effect. Purchase once; no equipment step is needed. At subsequent epoch settlement, both tracks gain throughput ×1.3, multiplying with other throughput factors without repeat-purchase stacking. Cards per epoch are rounded and capped at 4–320; offline simulation stays at 12 cards. Epoch duration is unchanged. Automatic configuration prefers ReLU; only one activation is used at a time. When selected, the concept board retains a base 98% of backward learning signals and 95% of forward signals per layer, compounded across layers; other active techniques can change these rates. Above the architecture depth tolerance, effective parameter retention per excess layer changes from 75% to 85% in ceiling and overfitting estimates; this has no effect within the tolerance. Train and evaluate to see scores; no instant score bonus.
