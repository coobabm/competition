# 经济压力与破产 · 改造计划

2026-10-08 · Status: approved by the user, being implemented.

## The user's request
- Stages 1–5 are very easy: casual training passes.
- Only stage 6 (Transformer / 全部放开) is hard. It needs items whose effects stack exponentially, otherwise it can't be done.
- Rent is ¥300 a day.
- Every training round costs a lot of electricity.
- Design further ways to lose money.
- Running out of money means failure: the computer gets sold.

## 1. Difficulty curve
- **Abilities 2–5:** both thresholds drop a lot. Any model that has had a few rounds of training on the default automatic configuration, plus the data the player picks up naturally from hand labelling and the one cheap pack per stage, gets there. Target: ability 5 within about 25 minutes of play for a player who just follows the to-do note. Items still discount, but they are optional.
- **Ability 6 「全部放开」:** an *effective-scale* requirement that is impossible without items.
  - effective scale = trained parameters × data × the product of every item multiplier owned;
  - the multipliers stack multiplicatively, which is where the exponential growth comes from;
  - the stage-6 items: 注意力 ×3, Transformer ×10, 预热 ×1.5, 残差 ×2, BatchNorm ×1.5, 机房 (compute ×8, which allows a larger model), 混合精度 ×2 (new, 2016-plausible as "半精度");
  - the threshold is set so that a player must own most of them. One missing big item (Transformer, 注意力 or 机房) makes it fail, and the to-do note says which multipliers are missing;
  - pre-training (the existing 6.2 step) is where this check happens: its plateau is the effective-scale ratio.
- Pacing target: stage 6 alone takes about as long as stages 1–5 together, about 25–35 minutes, mostly earning the money for the items while staying solvent.

## 3. Bankruptcy
- Money can go negative. While it is negative:
  - **Day 1:** the 房东 texts 「房租该交了」, and the to-do note puts 「赚钱交房租（欠 ¥x）」 on top;
  - **Day 2:** the 房东 warns 「再不交就别住了」, and the electricity is cut: training is blocked, hand labelling still works;
  - **Day 3 still negative:** the **卖掉电脑** failure ending.
- **The 卖电脑 ending:**
  - a short cutscene: the screen dims, 喵鱼 opens itself with the listing 「二手电脑 一台 · 含显卡 · 急出」, and a buyer offers;
  - the AI's last lines in YY, held to its current ability: 是/否 at stage 1, a sentence later. For example 「……要走了吗」 / 「我会被格式化吗」;
  - the inner voice, then a black screen: 「电脑卖了 ¥x。房租交上了。」
  - a game-over card with 重新开始.
- **The save:** game over marks the save as ended. The next start begins a new game. The old file is kept as a timestamped backup, as the earlier manual backup was, never deleted.
- The early game is protected: no bankruptcy before the first rent is due, and the player starts with enough money for the first few days.

## 4. Verification
- Bot: a normal-skill run must finish without going bankrupt. A deliberately careless run (training nonstop without labelling) must go bankrupt.
- Tests:
  - rent per day;
  - electricity per round;
  - tier pricing;
  - the bankruptcy timeline (day 1, 2, 3);
  - the ending sets the save state;
  - the stage-6 effective-scale rule: fails without items, passes with the full stack.
