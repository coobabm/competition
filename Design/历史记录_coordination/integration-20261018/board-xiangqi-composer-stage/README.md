# Restricted Xiangqi: generals and one rook, verified mate in one

Import `board-xiangqi-composer.patch` after `board-chess-composer-stage`. 11 paths. Static delimiter audit, no-drift check and production `git apply --check` pass. No C# compilation, Unity, models or tests executed here.

## Exact implemented scope

- A small pure 9×10 engine supports Red general, Black general and at most one Red rook. Generals remain in their actual three-by-three palaces and move one orthogonal intersection. They cannot face on an unobstructed file. Rook moves follow straight unobstructed lines; captures and check-escaping responses are validated. General capture is not a move shortcut; a checkmate requires the checked side to have **no legal response**, including a legal rook capture if available.
- A generator shuffles rook positions around a simple mirrored general arrangement. It rejects initial checks, examines actual legal Red moves and checks every legal Black reply. It publishes only positions with a real one-move checkmate. At most 90 candidate positions are examined. This is explicit restricted composition, not a full Xiangqi engine or an imported authored endgame bank.
- Game Hub shows **将帅车残局 / Rook endgame** only with purchased Lv4. It draws a 9×10 board with palace diagonals, marks pieces, allows real selection/moves, resets the same position, creates another verified puzzle, and clears work on save change. Page reopen retains the current board. UI says generals-and-one-rook mate in one, not full free play. No sample rewards, completion-prerequisite receipts, or fictitious packs are generated by composition/practice.
- Both this generator and the preceding chess generator now advance at most one candidate per **0.1 seconds while their own page is visible**; they pause while hidden. The small follow-up to chess also shortens English help to fit its existing area. Their UI helpers use elapsed-time deadlines instead of the earlier fixed frame count.
